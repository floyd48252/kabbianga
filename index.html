Welcome to Kabianga

A place to learn, grow and lead

Kabianga School is a public national boys’ boarding school in Belgut, Kericho County. Its long history of education, its focus on discipline and hard work, and its commitment to preparing learners for the future are part of the Kabianga experience.

The school’s message is to equip students with the skills they need to transform themselves, others, and the world. Alongside classroom learning, students can take part in sports, journalism, music, and performing arts.

Admissions

Preparing to join Kabianga School

Admission and placement are subject to the Ministry of Education and the school's current intake instructions.

This is a general preparation checklist, not an official admission letter. Follow the latest joining instructions issued for your child's intake, as forms and requirements may change.

What to prepare

  • The official Ministry placement/admission letter for Kabianga School.
  • Completed school joining and medical forms, as provided in the current joining instructions.
  • The learner's birth certificate and academic records, if requested by the school.
  • Parent or guardian contact details and any other documents specified in the admission letter.
  • Uniform, personal items, and fee arrangements according to the current school instructions.
